Macro Log privacy

Last updated November 2026

What Macro Log stores

Macro Log is a personal food, macro and weight tracker. Everything you log is stored in your browser on your own device. The app has no server and no accounts of its own, and the people who publish it cannot see your data.

Optional Google Drive sync

If you choose to connect Google Drive, Macro Log saves one file containing your log in a private application folder inside your own Google Drive, so your data can follow you between devices. The app requests only the permission to manage its own application data folder. It cannot see, read or change any other files in your Drive. You can disconnect at any time from Settings, and remove the app's data from Drive at any time under Google Drive's own settings for connected apps.

Sign-in is handled entirely by Google. Macro Log never sees your password, and the temporary access token Google issues is kept only in your browser.

Third-party food data

Barcode scanning and online food search send the barcode or search text you enter to Open Food Facts, and to USDA FoodData Central if you add your own key. No personal information is included in these requests.

Analytics and tracking

None. Macro Log contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
